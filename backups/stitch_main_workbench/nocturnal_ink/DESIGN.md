# Design System Specification: The Nocturnal Manuscript

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star: "The Digital Sanctuary"
The Creative North Star for this design system is **"The Digital Sanctuary."** Unlike standard development IDEs that are loud, cluttered, and technical, this system is designed to facilitate "Deep Flow." It treats the screen not as a monitor, but as a moonlit desk. 

By utilizing **intentional asymmetry** (e.g., weighted sidebars against a centered manuscript) and **tonal depth**, we break the "template" look. We prioritize the writer's psychology, using high-contrast typography for the prose and low-contrast UI elements to ensure the interface recedes into the background until needed.

---

## 2. Colors: Tonal Atmosphere
Our palette moves away from flat "Dark Mode" into a multi-dimensional, immersive environment.

### The Color Logic
*   **Primary (#75d1ff):** The "Ethereal Spark." Used for AI-driven insights and active focus states.
*   **Secondary (#ffb954):** The "Candlelight." Used for warm alerts and creative milestones.
*   **Neutral Foundation:** We use a "Deep Sea" hierarchy, ranging from `surface-container-lowest` (#0c0c20) for the editor heart to `surface-bright` (#37374d) for floating contextual menus.

### The "No-Line" Rule
**Explicit Instruction:** Prohibit the use of 1px solid borders to define sections. Layout boundaries must be established via:
1.  **Background Shifts:** The Activity Bar (`surface-container-low`) sits against the Sidebar (`surface-container`).
2.  **Shadow Depth:** A floating AI panel uses a large, diffused ambient shadow rather than a stroke.

### The "Glass & Gradient" Rule
To elevate the "literary" feel, use **Glassmorphism** for all floating overlays (e.g., AI suggestion popovers). Apply `surface-variant` with a 60% opacity and a 12px backdrop-blur. Main CTAs should utilize a subtle linear gradient from `primary` (#75d1ff) to `on_primary_container` (#0094c4) at a 135-degree angle to provide a "soulful" glow.

---

## 3. Typography: The Editorial Contrast
We employ a dual-font strategy to separate the "Act of Creation" from the "Tools of Management."

*   **The Manuscript (Noto Serif SC):** Used for `body-lg`, `title-lg`, and the main editor. Serif fonts evoke the history of literature and reduce eye strain during long-form writing.
*   **The Interface (Manrope / Noto Sans SC):** Used for `label-sm`, `headline-sm`, and all UI components. This sans-serif is clean, technical, and stays out of the way of the prose.

**Hierarchy Note:** 
`display-lg` (Manrope) is reserved for chapter titles and milestones, creating a sense of "Book Design" authority. `label-sm` is used for word counts and metadata to maintain a professional, minimalist footprint.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth: Tonal Layering
We do not use structural lines. We use **Tonal Layering** to convey hierarchy.

*   **The Layering Principle:** 
    *   **Level 0 (The Void):** `surface-container-lowest` (#0c0c20) is the editor—the deepest point of focus.
    *   **Level 1 (The Desk):** `surface` (#111125) is the main application shell.
    *   **Level 2 (The Tools):** `surface-container` (#1e1e32) is used for sidebars and tree views.
*   **Ambient Shadows:** Floating AI panels must use a shadow with a 24px blur, 0px offset, and 8% opacity of `on_surface` (#e2e0fc). 
*   **The Ghost Border:** If accessibility requires a container definition, use `outline-variant` at 15% opacity. Never use 100% opaque strokes.

---

## 5. Components: Refined Utility

### The Activity Bar (Vertical)
*   **Visuals:** No background color; icons sit directly on `surface-container-low`.
*   **Active State:** A `primary` (#75d1ff) vertical "pill" indicator (2px wide) on the left, paired with a soft glow effect on the icon.

### Tree Views (Story Structure)
*   **Style:** No vertical guidelines. Indentation (Spacing 4) defines hierarchy. 
*   **Selection:** Instead of a full-width background bar, use a "subtle lift" via `surface-container-highest` and a `title-sm` font weight change.

### Specialized AI Panels
*   **Style:** Semi-transparent Glassmorphism.
*   **Interaction:** Text generated by AI should "fade in" using a cyan-to-white gradient mask to simulate a dream-like materialization.

### Inputs & Text Areas
*   **Style:** Borderless. Use `surface-container-highest` for the background.
*   **Focus State:** A 1px "Ghost Border" of `primary` at 40% opacity and a subtle outer glow (4px blur).

### Buttons
*   **Primary:** Gradient fill (Primary to Primary-Container), `label-md` uppercase for a sophisticated, editorial feel. 
*   **Tertiary (Ghost):** No background, `primary` text. For use in low-emphasis toolbar actions.

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
*   **Do** use vertical white space (Spacing 8 or 10) to separate "Acts" or "Chapters" in the tree view instead of dividers.
*   **Do** ensure the editor area has a maximum line width (approx. 700px) to maintain readability, regardless of screen size.
*   **Do** use the `secondary` (Amber) color sparingly—only for moments where the writer's "internal logic" might be broken (e.g., plot holes, contradictory character names).

### Don't:
*   **Don't** use pure black (#000) or pure white (#FFF). It shatters the nocturnal immersion.
*   **Don't** use "Snapping" animations. Use "Ease-in-out" transitions (300ms) to mimic the fluidity of thought.
*   **Don't** ever use a solid 1px border between the sidebar and the editor. Let the shift from `surface-container` to `surface-container-lowest` do the work.
